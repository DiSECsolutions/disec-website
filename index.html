Cloud POS + ERP Platform

Every tool your F&B business needs — in one platform.

DiSEC replaces your scattered billing apps, spreadsheet inventories, and manual reports with a single cloud-native system designed for restaurant and café operators.

Core POS

Blazing-Fast Order Entry

Touch-optimised interface works on tablet, desktop, or dedicated POS hardware. Build modifiers, combos, and variants without limits.

Inventory

Live Inventory Management

Auto-deduct stock on every sale. Set low-stock alerts, track ingredient-level consumption, and generate purchase orders automatically.

Payments

All Payment Modes, One Interface

UPI, card swipe, NFC, cash, and split-bill — with auto-generated GST-compliant KOT receipts and digital bill sharing via WhatsApp.

Analytics

Business Intelligence Dashboard

Daily sales summaries, hourly heatmaps, top-selling items, staff performance, and table turnover — available on your phone the moment a shift ends.

Staff & Tables

Table & Staff Management

Floor plan builder, table status view, captain-login per shift, and role-based access so every staff member sees only what they need.

Cloud ERP

Multi-Branch ERP Backbone

Centralise procurement, vendor management, expense tracking, and financial reporting across every outlet from a single HQ console.

How It Works

Live in minutes. ROI from day one.

No complex installations. No IT team required. DiSEC is designed to onboard a restaurant in under an hour.

01

Sign Up & Set Up Menu

Import your menu via CSV or build it in our visual editor. Add categories, modifiers, taxes and pricing in minutes.

02

Connect Your Hardware

Plug in any thermal printer, cash drawer, or QR display. Our app runs on iPad, Android tablet, or any browser.

03

Start Taking Orders

Your team is live. Every order flows to the kitchen display, inventory deducts, and payment is collected instantly.

04

Grow with Insights

AI-generated daily summaries, smart reorder suggestions, and customer preference trends guide your next decisions.

R&D Lab

Beyond POS — pioneering the intelligence layer.

While the POS platform powers daily operations, our research team is building the next stratum: custom AI models, purpose-built IoT hardware, and embedded intelligence that will make restaurant operations self-optimising.

AI Research

Custom AI Models for F&B

Training domain-specific language and time-series models on restaurant telemetry: demand forecasting, menu optimisation, and waste reduction at the item level.

Hardware

Proprietary POS Hardware

Designing compact, fanless POS terminals and kitchen display units with embedded edge compute — purpose-built for the humidity, grease, and noise of a commercial kitchen.

IoT

IoT Sensor Integration

Smart temperature sensors for cold-chain compliance, occupancy sensors for table management, and energy monitors — feeding live data back into the ERP layer.
